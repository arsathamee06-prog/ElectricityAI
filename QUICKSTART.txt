🧠 ML MODELS INCLUDED:

1. ARIMA
   • Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average
   • Best for: Linear trends, simpler patterns
   • Speed: ⚡⚡⚡ Fast

2. SARIMA
   • Seasonal ARIMA
   • Best for: Data with seasonal patterns
   • Speed: ⚡⚡ Medium

3. PROPHET
   • Facebook's Prophet forecasting library
   • Best for: Robust, high-level patterns
   • Speed: ⚡⚡ Medium

4. LSTM
   • Long Short-Term Memory neural network
   • Best for: Complex patterns, highest accuracy
   • Speed: ⚡ Slower (but most accurate)

System automatically selects BEST model! ✓

🔍 PREPARE YOUR DATA:

Your CSV should have these columns:
  • timestamp (or date): YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS format
  • demand (or consumption): Numeric values

Example:
  timestamp,demand
  2024-01-01 00:00:00,85.2
  2024-01-01 01:00:00,82.5
  ...

Minimum: 100 data points
Recommended: 6+ months of data
